Exploration B: bold playful

Saturated color on warm paper, ink outlines with a solid ledge underneath, and one shape per difficulty. The identity has no mascot; the two o's in the name are eyes, and that's the only face we use. Screens are 390 by 844 CSS px. Every screen below renders from the same markup in both themes.

Fredoka + Atkinson Hyperlegible NextInk outlines, 4 px ledgesPebble, cookie, burst, targetSprings, not tweens

1. Home

The Daily is the only big thing on the page. Streak and practice sit under it, and nothing above the fold asks for an account.

2. Mystery, typing a guess

Mystery 2 at clue 2, after a wrong guess on clue 1. The keyboard is up, so the layout keeps the current clue, the field and four suggestions above it. What the clue is worth sits on the clue itself.

3. Practice, right after answering

Wrong pick shakes and turns tomato with an X and "Your answer". The right one pops green with a check and "Correct answer". The others sink flat but keep readable text. The explanation sheet rises over empty space, so nothing shifts.

4. Daily results

Opens on the best fact (a personal best), then the percentile labelled "so far today", then how everyone did and one row per round. One primary button ends the session.

5. Share preview image (1200 by 630)

The path, the score and the Encore error. Never an answer. Shown at half size here.
